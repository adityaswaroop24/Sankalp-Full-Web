The Challenge

Construction projects are harder to manage than they should be.

Home construction involves multiple people, changing requirements, material purchases, expenses and daily site activities. Without a centralized system, important information can easily get lost.

📋

Scattered Information

Project information is often spread across phone calls, messages, notebooks and spreadsheets.

💸

Budget Uncertainty

Homeowners can struggle to understand where money is being spent and whether the project is staying within budget.

👷

Coordination Problems

Coordinating contractors, engineers, workers and homeowners can become difficult without a centralized communication system.

The Sankalp Solution

Everything your construction project needs.

Sankalp provides a single digital environment where every important part of your construction project can be organized and monitored.
